Biography

Exploring physics
through computation.

I am an undergraduate physics student at Yachay Tech University in Ecuador. My research experience spans computational condensed matter physics, relativistic scattering, astrophysical data analysis, and quantum photonics.

In the CompNano Research Group, I work on covalent triazine frameworks using first-principles density functional theory and machine-learned force fields. During a research internship at the Korea Institute of Science and Technology (KIST), I worked on FDTD design and optimization of nanophotonic devices, including cavities for silicon W-center emitters, quantum-dot extraction structures, and focused grating couplers.

I am also the first author of a study of superradiance in an α-attractor potential using the log-derivative method. Across these projects, computational methods are a common thread in how I explore physical systems.
